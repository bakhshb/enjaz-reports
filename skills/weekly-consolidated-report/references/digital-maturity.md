# Digital-experience model

All digital PowerPoint tables, including every detail table and challenge table, must use native RTL direction. Preserve the review JSON's PDF field order (`أساس التقييم | الإنجاز | المهمة | م`); map those values into the corresponding RTL table columns without changing the JSON schema. Numbering/codes appear at the right, and Latin task codes retain LTR text direction inside the RTL table: each code is written as its own run tagged `en-US` (PowerPoint lays out Arabic-tagged runs right-to-left and does not reliably honour bidi control characters). Validate direction independently of paragraph alignment.

Use this reference only after the user chooses **التقرير الموحد مع نضج التجربة الرقمية**. The added summary follows the transactions summary and precedes the detail divider. The divider's «تفاصيل مؤشر نضج التجربة الرقمية» button opens the populated indicator-detail slides after the task and Suhail details; the detail data must not appear only in the summary. The current model remains separate.

## Source contract: the landscape PDF

The current digital-maturity PDF is a required fourth input and arrives as the landscape («horizontal») export. It is a **data source only**; its layout, page breaks and page count are never reproduced.

- **Page one** holds the four perspective percentages (each card's headline value is the percentage in its largest type; a smaller weekly-change line under it is ignored), one sector bar row per agency/center (a gray bar with the note inside the label's parentheses — read in either logical or mirrored order — marks a sector that is not assessed separately), and the notes table (`# | المنظور | …`, titled «التحديات (آخر تحديث: …)» in the source).
- **Every later page belongs to one sector.** A sector may span several consecutive pages titled «القطاع (1/3)», «القطاع (2/3)», …; a continued section heading on a later page reads «المنظور: 15% (تتمة)»; a sector's challenges table («التحديات» or «أبرز التحديثات») may appear on any of its pages. All of these are **source pagination artifacts**. The unit of data is the sector, not the page.
- Pages without a «(k/n)» suffix are single-page sectors. Sectors keep their source order.
- The committed sample `tests/fixtures/digital-maturity-horizontal.pdf` is the approved source layout (16 pages: summary plus 3+2+1+3+6 sector pages). The approved **output** is `assets/weekly-report-digital-reference.pdf`, the user-approved report of 5 November 2026 exported from PowerPoint: one sector per contiguous run of slides, output-only «(k/n)», «(تتمة)» at the top of a continuation, then «أبرز التحديثات» and «التحديات» once at the end of the sector (an empty one omitted), and a single «أبرز التحديثات» table on the summary slide. Compare a generated report with the reference for design, never for values.

## Updates and challenges (رقمي)

The source gives each sector one notes table («أبرز التحديثات» or «التحديات») and page one a single «التحديات» table. Its title does not decide the output. During review, the agent:

1. **Classifies** every source note by its content: progress, a completed step, an expectation or work under way → `updates` (أبرز التحديثات / التحديث); a delay, an obstacle, a dependency, a missing decision or no response → `challenges` (التحديات / التحدي). A note that mixes both is split into one update and one challenge. Every source note must land in at least one list; none is dropped.
2. **Rewrites** each item in formal government Arabic, the way a ministry officer writes a status line: فصحى رسمية in short declarative sentences (جمل خبرية قصيرة), one fact per sentence, passive or neutral voice («اعتمد…»، «يجري العمل على…»، «تأخر…»). No colloquial words (الحين، شغالين، وش، اللي، عشان، يبي…), no judgement or adjective the source does not state, no rhetorical openers. Follow «Wording of التحديث and التحدي» below. An **update** (التحديث) has no title: the row is the statement itself, e.g. «اكتمل نقل محتوى نسك عمرة إلى nusuk.sa، ويبقى موعد الإطلاق 15 ديسمبر.», never «نقل المحتوى: اكتمل…». This applies to the summary «أبرز التحديثات» rows and to every sector's updates. A **challenge** (التحدي) keeps the form «عنوان قصير: النص» (e.g. «تأخر التقدم: لم يسجل أي تقدم في الملف حتى الآن.»). The builder does not stop for an update that starts with a short label and a colon, or for a note with one of the listed colloquial words; it records a wording notice for each. Keep every percentage, count, date and named entity (منصة، وكالة، جهة). **Remove task codes** (`INF-02`, `S-02`, `CS-52`, `INF-05..07`) and say what they refer to in words (e.g. «أربعة متطلبات»); keep dates such as «4 نوفمبر 2026» or «28 أكتوبر». Add no fact that is not in the PDF. A note that still carries a task code gets a wording notice too. When the notices file has wording notices about text you wrote yourself (a title on an update, a colloquial word, a task code, a machine-sounding phrase), correct the review file and rebuild once without asking the user, then report what remains. A card status that is not «معلق» or «لا ينطبق» is kept as written with a status notice; check it against the PDF page.

   **Wording of التحديث and التحدي.** Both cells are read by the Minister, so each must be exact and professional:

   - Start with the verb that says what happened: «اكتمل»، «اعتمد»، «سلّم»، «نشر» for what is done; «يجري»، «تستكمل»، «تناقش» for what is under way; «تأخر»، «لم يصدر»، «لم يغلق» for what is late. Prefer the direct verb to «تم + مصدر» («اعتمدت الخطة», not «تم اعتماد الخطة») when the sentence allows it. When a source sentence is quoted as written and uses «تم», keep it and fix only its agreement («تمت مشاركة…»).
   - Name who and when if the source does: the entity, the platform, the date, the percentage, the count. Write quantities as the source gives them («أربعة متطلبات»، «80%»، «15 ديسمبر»). Do not round or soften («نحو»، «قرابة») and do not replace a named entity with «الجهة المعنية».
   - One fact per sentence, two sentences at most per row. A second point is a second line (it becomes a bullet).
   - A **challenge** states the obstacle, then what it holds up or what is needed, in that order: «قرار توحيد مركز الاتصال: لم يصدر القرار حتى تاريخه، ويتوقف عليه اعتماد كراسة مركز الاتصال.» Its short title names the subject (two to four words), not a judgement.
   - Leave out every phrase the source did not say: no importance («محوري»، «حيوي»، «جوهري»، «رائد»), no added consequence («مما يسهم في…»، «ما يعكس…»، «بما يعزز…»), no lead-in («تجدر الإشارة»، «في إطار»، «في ظل»، «حرصا على»), no «رغم … إلا أن», no summary line, no vague source («يرى المختصون»). Say the fact itself.
   - Keep one name for one thing: if the row says «منصة نسك», do not call it «المنصة الرقمية» in the next sentence.
   - Plain text: no em or en dashes, no emoji, no markup.

   | Instead of | Write |
   | --- | --- |
   | «يشهد ملف الذكاء الاصطناعي تقدما ملحوظا، حيث تم تسليمه ومن المتوقع اعتماده قريبا.» | «سلّم ملف الذكاء الاصطناعي، ويتوقع اعتماده هذا الأسبوع.» |
   | «تحديات في الربط: رغم الجهود المبذولة إلا أن الربط يواجه بعض التحديات.» | «الربط بالنفاذ الوطني الموحد: لم يكتمل ربط جميع المنصات، وتناقش المعالجة مع الوكيل المساعد للتحول الرقمي.» |
   | «تم العمل على نشر الشروط والأحكام مما يسهم في تعزيز الشفافية.» | «نشرت الشروط والأحكام في منصة نسك وتطبيقها.» |

   The builder lists what it finds of these patterns as wording notices (`report_common/wording.py`); reword those rows and rebuild. «رحلة» is not on the list: «رحلة الحاج» and «رحلة المستفيد» are the ministry's own terms.
3. Leaves a list empty when the sector has nothing of that kind; its table is then **omitted**, not shown empty.

The summary slide's table is titled **«أبرز التحديثات»** with the header `# | المنظور | التحديث` (1–4 rows from page one, `updates` key), rewritten under the same rules. A row whose text spans two points is written as two lines; each line renders as a native bullet inside the cell.

`scripts/digital_maturity.py` reads the landscape pages, restores logical Arabic from the visual text layer (Latin codes, digits, percentages and brackets included), and folds each sector's pages into one record set. The JSON keys are `source_pdf_sha256`, `source_date_hijri`, `metrics` (`experience`, `satisfaction`, `complaints`, `tools`), `sectors` (name, four metrics, optional note), summary `source_updates` (page one's notes as read, perspective and text; evidence only), `updates` (the summary table rows; drafted as a copy of `source_updates` and rewritten during review), `detail_sectors` (one entry per sector: `title` without suffix, `source_pages`, `cards`, four-column `rows` including section headings but no «(تتمة)», `source_notes` (every note from all of the sector's pages with its source table title, evidence only) and the reviewer-filled `updates` and `challenges` lists) and `reviewed`. The extractor begins with `reviewed: false` and empty `updates`/`challenges` per sector because the PDF text layer can still reverse glyphs, lose characters as `(cid:…)`, or wrap codes, and because classification and rewriting are review work. Compare every field with the rendered source pages, correct the JSON, classify and rewrite the notes (see «Updates and challenges» above), then set `reviewed` to `true`. The builder rejects a sector whose source notes were not classified. If one row or note is illegible or ambiguous, do not stop the whole report for it: keep what the rendered page shows as far as it can be read, never invent the rest, and name the page and row in the final reply so the user can supply a clearer source. Stop only when the PDF as a whole cannot be read. Do not infer runtime data from master examples.

The master's detail slides are **design patterns only**: the first one supplies the title strip, the header row, one section-heading row per perspective (its fill color), the body row, the four cards and the challenge table. Never carry sample percentages, rows, or challenge prose from the PowerPoint master into a generated report. The master summary still contains sample values that differ from the supplied PDF; the PDF controls runtime values. Preserve the master's fixed «آلية الاحتساب» note between the four indicators and sector chart, including its wording and emphasis; the source's own «أساس الاحتساب» line is not extracted.

## Output pagination (portrait slides)

The builder re-flows every sector with the same continuous-flow rules used for task and Suhail details:

- One sector starts on a fresh detail slide; its records fill the slide down to the footer clearance. The number of digital detail slides follows the data, not the number of source pages.
- Records are never split, and a section heading never ends a slide. When a section continues on the next slide, the continuation starts with the same heading plus «(تتمة)» (generated from the output pagination, never copied from the source).
- A sector that needs more than one slide titles them «القطاع (1/n)» … «القطاع (n/n)» where *n* is the number of **output** slides; a single-slide sector has no suffix. The four cards repeat with identical values on every slide of the sector.
- The sector's «أبرز التحديثات / التحديث» table, then its «التحديات / التحدي» table, appear **once**, directly below the last records of the sector, each after the small approved gap. A table whose list is empty is omitted. If they cannot fit under the last record, the trailing record moves with them to the next slide; a slide never holds only these tables. If they cannot follow that record even on a fresh slide, they are set in smaller text there (not below 8 pt) and a notice asks for shorter wording.
- The task/indicator column keeps the master's fixed header; the source's «المؤشر» header is not transferred.
- Surplus master detail slides are removed; the remaining block stays contiguous after the Suhail details and before the thank-you slide, and the divider button targets its first slide.

The four perspectives of the summary slide are fixed: its cards and the sector chart always show تجربة المستخدم، رضا المستفيد، التعامل مع الشكاوى، التقنيات والأدوات, and new data never adds or removes one. What grows and shrinks is the «أبرز التحديثات» table at the bottom of that slide: it has as many rows as the reviewed summary `updates`, with no upper limit; extra rows copy its first body row and fewer rows remove the rest. New sectors are added to the sector chart. In the detail slides, a section heading that names no perspective (e.g. «خدمات المستفيدين»، «الجهات المساندة») takes the previous heading's fill, or the sector's first perspective with a percentage, so a new kind of section never stops the build. The digital summary stays on one slide; if its measured table does not fit it shrinks like the other summaries (down to 8 pt). Past that the build still does not stop and there is no appendix for this table: the rows are squeezed into the space left, and a notice names the slide to check and asks for shorter updates in the review file, which you then write and rebuild. An empty detail card is kept empty with a notice. A section heading with no records under it is dropped from the slide with a notice. The four perspective values and sector percentages are independent rates, not portions that must sum to 100. Preserve every number as shown. A missing perspective for one sector stays blank. A sector marked with a note has a labeled gray bar and no invented percentages. Keep the summary updates' perspectives in source order.
